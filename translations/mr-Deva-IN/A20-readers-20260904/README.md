OpenStax Intermediate Algebra 2e - Marathi reading package, 4 September 2026.

Start with [the reader index](index.html). This is a coherent **two-section package**, not a complete textbook or a completed five-book programme.

- **Relations and Functions, m81373:** complete recovered source assembly; semantic HTML and the unchanged 64-page PDF.
- **Graphs of Functions, m81374:** complete recovered source assembly including its source review/test material; newly completed semantic HTML and repaired PDF.
- **21 optional study readers, MR-BRIDGE-002–022:** smaller overlapping study chunks, not 21 additional sections. The full m81373 reader uses the complete MR011 replacement rather than the older shortened MR001 selections.

The two full modules retain 1,991 canonical source IDs and 339 source exercises. The package distinguishes 196 source-supplied answers, 29 recovered author-added answers to source omissions in m81373, and 114 explicitly unanswered source exercises in m81374. It does not present added answers as OpenStax source content.

## Purpose and reading support

The inherited design bridges Grade 8 mathematics to first-year STEM study in Marathi. Prerequisite checks, worked explanations, source exercise/answer navigation, original figures with Marathi descriptions and clearly labelled additions are retained. Figures may retain English labels; their Marathi descriptions and reading keys are part of the text. HTML is offline and responsive, with embedded images and keyboard-accessible tables. PDFs are companion reading copies, not claims of PDF/UA or universal assistive-technology compatibility.
