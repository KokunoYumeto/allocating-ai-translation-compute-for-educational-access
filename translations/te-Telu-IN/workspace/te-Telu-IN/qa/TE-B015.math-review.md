## Carry reconciliation and source quirks

The checker uses integer division and remainder only. Each column total is the sum of the actual addend digits plus its incoming carry. Writing the remainder modulo ten and carrying the quotient preserves the total value. Carry is a count of next-place units, not the same absolute value as the small digit itself.

- 43+69: ones total 12 writes 2 and carries one ten; final eleven tens occupy both remaining result positions, producing 112. The source's literal instruction to write 11 is preserved.
- 324+586: column totals 10, 11, 9 produce digits 0, 1, 9 from right to left. Exactly ten requires a carry; zero in the ones place cannot be omitted. Original four image stages are blank result, `0`, `10`, and `910`.
- 1,683+479: totals 12, 16, 11, 2 produce partials `2`, `62`, `162`, `2,162`. At the hundreds-stage source MathML, the new carry above thousands is not drawn until the final frame. The source is retained exactly; complete reasoning must state that pending carry. The tens addition is written 1+7+8 in the source, which is numerically equivalent to incoming carry plus the addend digits in top-to-bottom order.

The source partials are `4`, `14`, `814`, **`0814`**, and final `30,814`. The zero in `0814` records the thousands position already processed; it is neither a new leading zero on the completed answer nor disposable padding. The shorter addends have zero digits in missing high places.
