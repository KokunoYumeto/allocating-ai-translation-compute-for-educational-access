## Telugu and English grammar decisions

The source's math fragments fix the order of individual numeric nodes and
sometimes supply no text slot after the last operand. Telugu therefore
uses complete functional nominal phrases around those fixed slots:

- “1కు కలపవలసిన సంఖ్య2” for1plus2.
- “మొత్తం కనుగొనవలసిన సంఖ్యలు3మరియు4” for the sum of3and4.
- “5ను పెంచవలసిన పరిమాణం6” for5increased by6.
- “8ను కలపవలసిన ఆధార సంఖ్య7” for8more than7.
- “6ను చేర్చవలసిన ఆధార సంఖ్య4” for6added to4.

The added bridge supplies the natural alternative forms “7కంటే8ఎక్కువైన
సంఖ్య” and “4కు6చేర్చితే” and identifies each base/added quantity.
No MathML operands are moved merely to imitate Telugu syntax.

English words remain recognizable in the words column with Telugu glosses.
The worked explanation's italicized `sum`, `of`, `and` and
`increased by` are explicitly labeled English. In particular, the
catalog does not replace English “of” with a claimed Telugu suffix rule.
The source addends remain19and23, with their actual node order.

Functional terms continue: గణిత రాత (expression), కలిపే సంఖ్యలు
(addends), మొత్తం (sum), సమానత్వాన్ని తెలిపే వాక్యం (equation),
and writing with mathematical signs for math notation. They are
defined in context, not certified as official regional equivalents.

“More than” is context-sensitive. The original bridge contrasts
