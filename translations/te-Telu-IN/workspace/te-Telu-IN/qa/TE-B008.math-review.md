  questions are not whole or counting numbers; 0 is whole but not counting
  under this source convention. Accept an equivalent set in any order.
- The source says “place value” but supplies place names. Preserve that answer
  convention and keep added contributions separate. In `fs-id2466117` partc,
  the 0 in579,601 is in tens and contributes0, not10. In `fs-id2197876` parte,
  the 0 in56,804,379 is in ten thousands and contributes0, not10,000.
- Preserve international groups such as078,023,005,008,044,017,036 and every
  trailing000 group. In particular, `fs-id1572155` is3,500,000,000,000 dollars
  and `fs-id1733890` is39,000,000,000,000 gallons. Units are not converted.
- All 30 rounding parts were recomputed by integer quotient/remainder, then
  checked against adjacent-multiple distances. None is an exact tie. A
  controlling5 in163,584,18,549, the hundred-million population question or
  the million-kilometer question is above the midpoint, not exactly halfway.
- `fs-id1806959` rounds149,597,888 to100,000,000 at hundred millions and to
  150,000,000 at both ten millions and millions. Parts b/c have the same
  answer but different targets and controlling digits9/5; answer-only tests
  cannot verify those questions correctly.
- Round every original amount independently. `fs-id2792494` nearest1,000
  is24,000, not the25,000 obtained by first rounding24,493 to24,500.
  `fs-id1604312` nearest100 is18,500, not the18,600 obtained by first rounding

- In `fs-id1185521`, both hundreds targets are9. The adjacent9 in63,994 causes
  a carry to64,000; adjacent4 in63,949 leaves63,900. A target9 alone does not
  imply carrying.
- Source answers spell “Thirty seven”, “Twenty three” and “Twenty four”
  without their usual hyphens. Those literal forms are recorded separately
  from standardized English numeric references; they are not arithmetic
  errors or Telugu suffix rules.
- Naming dates are context, not extra problem parts: 2016 in the China
  forecast and July1,2014 in the India estimate. Other relative dates and
  projections remain historical source wording, not current claims.
  613,200hours=70*365*24 and525,600minutes=365*24*60 assume365-day years.
