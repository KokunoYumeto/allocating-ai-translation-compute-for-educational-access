  English convention initially said rounding is not exact equality to the
  original quantity without qualification. Exact multiples0 and4,000 correctly
  remain unchanged elsewhere in the bridge. Final wording now says a rounded
  value need not equal the original and an exact multiple stays unchanged.

  During finalization the translator spelled out groups whose value

  reading of “9 thousand groups.” The final assembled paragraph explicitly
  describes one group worth1,000 plus nine such groups, regrouped as one group
  worth10,000 and zero groups worth1,000. The numerical sequence remains
  1,9,10,1,0,1,2,0 with unchanged MathML. This is a count/unit clarification,
  not changed arithmetic.
