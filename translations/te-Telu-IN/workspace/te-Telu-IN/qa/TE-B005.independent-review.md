## Content checks

- **Coverage and preservation — PASS.** There are 55 source slots: 40 localized
  and 15 protected/pass-through. The in-memory target retains all 97 ordered
  elements and all 57 source IDs. Independently compared all six MathML trees,
  including numeric/currency text, attributes and internal tails: no changes.
  Prose outside MathML is not mistaken for a mathematical token. All three media
  nodes map to the recorded localized assets.
- **English names and number groups — PASS.** Independently parsed the retained
  English prompt names to numbers: `53,401,742`, `9,246,073,189`, `53,809,051`
  and `2,022,714,466`. Telugu group names and coefficients express the same
  quantities. The initial period may have fewer than three digits; each later
  period has three. `standard form` is explicitly defined here as ordinary
  numeral form, not scientific notation. International commas remain unchanged.
- **017 accessibility correction — PASS, disclosed upstream discrepancy.**
  Frozen media `fs-id2903601` incorrectly associates “seventy-three thousand”
  with 742. Reinspected the preserved original JPEG: it visibly shows 0, 7, 3.
  The canonical answer is `9,246,073,189`, and the following source paragraph
  explicitly explains the hundred-thousands placeholder. Telugu slot `s025`
  correctly describes 073 and the complete number. Catalog editorial metadata
  and the asset manifest document the correction; the frozen source and JPEG
  are unchanged. The other figure's legitimate 742 group is not altered.
- **Zero positions — PASS.** The source explanation correctly locates the 0 in
  073 at the hundred-thousands position of the full number. The first Try It
  retains 809 and 051: their zeros are respectively at ten-thousands and
  hundreds. The second retains 022 in the millions period: the leading zero is
  at hundred-millions, not an instruction to change 22 million into 220 million.
  The 204-million solution keeps its zero at ten-millions. A zero contribution
  is not confused with removing a digit position or a whole period.
- **Money, units and approximations — PASS.** The budget remains approximately
  `$77,000,000,000`, with the three unnamed periods filled as
  `77 | 000 | 000 | 000`. The Earth–Mars exercise remains approximately
  `34,000,000` miles; the carrier exercise remains `204,000,000` pounds.
  The bridge expressly treats these as source exercise givens, not current
  measurements or a claim about all carriers. It preserves the approximate
  qualifier when interpreting the Mars answer and explains that writing more
  digits does not create a new exact measurement. No rupee, kilometre or
  kilogram conversion is introduced.
- **English grammar and regional scope — PASS.** The source's unnamed-ones
  convention is explicitly located in English number naming. The bridge does
  not apply English final-s deletion to Telugu words or invent a Telugu
  conjunction prohibition. The English names remain intact as bilingual
  references; Telugu connecting forms are explained separately. Million/billion
  loanwords are editorial, not claimed official TS/AP vocabulary. The Indian
  notation comparison is labeled an addition and does not replace source
  grouping.
- **Bridge arithmetic and routing — PASS.** Evaluated all 16 actual numeric
  equality chains, including both intermediate equalities in the two long
  source-solution expansions; all sides agree. The bridge has 36 unique IDs and
  12 fragment links, all resolving to bridge or source IDs. Four entry items,
  four rechecks and four full source Try It explanations are present. Telugu
  and English routing agree: K1 uses D01–D02/R01–R02; K2 uses D03–D04/R03–R04;
  each skill requires both recheck items correct with reasons. Thresholds are
  explicitly editorial teaching rules, not validated grade-placement results.
