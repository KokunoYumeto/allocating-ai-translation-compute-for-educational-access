## B012 name, grouping and zero reasoning

The Telugu input is మూడు వందల నలభై రెండు వేల ఆరు. The original English
phrase `three hundred forty-two thousand six` is retained as a labeled gloss;
no English suffix or absent-and convention becomes a Telugu grammar rule.
The source solution's three tokens remain342,comma,006, not342,600 or3426.

The bridge explains342×1,000=342,000 and the last group's
0×100+0×10+6×1=6. Combining342 | 006 gives342,000+6=342,006. It explicitly
distinguishes006 (six) from600 (six hundred). This is an exact numeral
conversion, not rounding or a new external measurement claim.

Independent prose review identified an ambiguity in the initial phrase
“342 వేల సమూహాలు”: it could be read as342,000 groups. With the parent's
explicit authorization, only that B012 bridge phrase was changed to
“వెయ్యి విలువగల 342 సమూహాలు” (342 groups, each worth one thousand).
The equation, answer, catalog and all other B012 prose remain unchanged.
