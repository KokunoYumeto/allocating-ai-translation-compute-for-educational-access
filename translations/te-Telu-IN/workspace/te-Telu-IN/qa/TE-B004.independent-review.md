## English rule scope and Telugu phrasing

- The introductory translation explicitly applies final-s removal to English
  period names, with `millions -> million` as an example. It expressly rejects
  deleting a Telugu final letter. The bridge repeats this scope for the four
  named English scales.
- The *and* sentence identifies the English word and the source's English naming
  convention. It does not create a rule prohibiting Telugu conjunctions or claim
  all varieties of English must follow one convention.
- Standalone Telugu glosses such as `ముప్పై ఏడు మిలియన్లు` are distinguished
  from connected full-name forms such as `ముప్పై ఏడు మిలియన్ల`. The text does
  not derive Telugu morphology by mechanically removing English letters.
- The ones-period name is omitted from completed names. The zero-number boundary
  is handled explicitly: a wholly zero period can be unspoken inside a larger
  number, but the number0 itself is named `సున్నా / zero`.
- The bridge identifies million/billion/trillion loanwords and its wording for
  period as editorial bilingual choices. Native review and AP evidence remain
  open; the source canon is not claimed to attest these loanword spellings.
