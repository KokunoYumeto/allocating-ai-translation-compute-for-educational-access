All are in the preserved original and new SVG. Future target alt should cover them.

The large addition table genuinely has 11 columns; do not apply the exception there.

The carry over thousands appears in the final row. Indonesian preserves the same structure. This is not a raster problem, and this task did not change it. Preserve source MathML and clarify the carried thousand in separate instructional prose if needed; do not claim it is already visible in that intermediate source row.
