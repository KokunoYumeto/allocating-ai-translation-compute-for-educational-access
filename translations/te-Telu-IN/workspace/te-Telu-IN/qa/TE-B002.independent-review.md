  allowed text change is `Sum = ` to `మొత్తం = ` inside `mtext`. Prose outside
  each MathML root is correctly excluded from this math-only comparison.
  `base-10` remains a source math-text label and is explained in Telugu prose.
- **Five-column tables — PASS.** Both `fs-id1714120` (138) and `fs-id1785447`
  (215) contain five rows including the header and five cells per row. In both
  languages the three data rows preserve digit/group counts, unit values

  not silently changed in the source. Telugu descriptions, and the English
  reader's disclosed display descriptions, correctly say five columns.
  `B002-table-guide` explains that the source's “Place value” column contains
  place names; it distinguishes group count, unit value and positional
