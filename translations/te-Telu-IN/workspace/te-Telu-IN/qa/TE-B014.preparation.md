   summarizes the illustrated steps rather than exhaustively listing all
   rows; include the fifth equation/conclusion rows if supplying explicit
   target row counts.

   and an equation such as3+4=7. The selected canon does not attest a formal

   Numbers** but supplies no linked file or URL in this selection. It remains
   source text, not an acquired offline worksheet. Do not imply this asset
   extraction acquired that activity or invent its contents.
