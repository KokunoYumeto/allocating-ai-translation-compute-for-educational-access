- `fs-id2642858`: coordinate is the number paired with a point, not the point
  or a two-dimensional ordered pair. నిరూపకం is explicitly provisional with
  its English gloss and learner-visible uncertainty notice.
- `fs-id2935860` and `fs-id4338000`: counting numbers start at 1; whole numbers
  add 0. The integer label is not substituted. The bridge correctly states the
  inclusion relation and discloses other natural-number conventions.
- `fs-id2711214`: rightward numbers increase, leftward numbers decrease. No
  unsupported universal claim about unit spacing between arbitrary labels.
- `fs-id3330600`: origin is the point labeled 0. మూలబిందువు remains provisional,
  not asserted to be an official regional term.
- `fs-id1336470`: a digit's positional contribution depends on position. The
  added 30-versus-3 example explains rather than conflates this with coordinate.

  explicit lesson context. The original bridge correctly limits specified-place
  rounding to one kind of estimation. Its row-count example distinguishes exact
  multiplication under an assumption from an unverified exact physical total.
