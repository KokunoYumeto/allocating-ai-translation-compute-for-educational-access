# Working Javanese reference shelf

Fifty official KBJI entries from Balai Bahasa Provinsi DIY were downloaded as
native HTML and converted to readable UTF-8 text under ignored
`downloads/jv-Latn-ID/canon/`. No PDF or DOCX was selected, so OCR is not needed.
Raw and readable hashes are in `sources.lock.json`. C01–C18 formed the initial
shelf; C19–C23 extend place-value and arithmetic-operation vocabulary, and
C24–C28 support large-number components and spelling. C29–C30 support bracket
and open/close vocabulary. C31–C34 address rounding and power vocabulary,
including negative evidence against a superficially similar rounding word.
C35–C36 support addition wording and distinguish ordinary storage from the
still-provisional mathematical carry label. C37–C39 address subtraction, numeric
reduction and the ordinary borrowing analogy used in source regrouping.
C40–C42 support the distinct area, length and width vocabulary needed by the
whole multiplication module; the mathematical definitions remain source-bound.
C43 supports ordinary replacement wording for evaluating an expression.
C44–C45 address remainder vocabulary and distinguish leftover senses from
specifically division-related usage in the actual returned entries.
C46–C47 test the proposed like-term wording: ordinary suku and jinis attestations
do not establish the algebraic meaning of suku sajinis or the coefficient loan.
This is a **small lexical
and usage canon**, not a collection of
Javanese mathematics textbooks and not an assertion
that this dictionary standardizes an academic mathematics register.

Read the relevant local entry at each production stage: terminology selection,
translation, narration, then final QA. Log what it changed or confirmed in
`CONSULTATIONS.md`. A citation list alone is not evidence of consultation. Expand
the shelf when a new topic needs evidence; do not repeat an unproductive search
loop. Newly drafted mathematical compounds must be marked as drafts rather than
retroactively called attested language.

Dictionary copyright and attribution remain with the named publisher. Only brief
reference quotations and our decision notes are committed. Full fetched pages
remain ignored consultation copies; there is no bulk dictionary redistribution.
The KBJI interface itself warns that generated pronunciation may not represent
the correct Javanese dialect; it is not evidence of approved audio.
