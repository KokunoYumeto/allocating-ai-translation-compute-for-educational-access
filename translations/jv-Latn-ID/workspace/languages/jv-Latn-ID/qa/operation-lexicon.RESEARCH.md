# Targeted operation lexicon research

Date: 2026-08-30. Purpose: improve the current A00/A10 translation and AX-2
narration drafts. This is not a general audit, a new training corpus, or native
language approval. Only this research note was written; no canon registry,
lockfile, translation, audio fixture, or build was changed.

## Method and limits

Read the official KBJI primary result pages, operated by Balai Bahasa Provinsi
Daerah Istimewa Yogyakarta. Read the actual definitions and relevant homographs,
not just search snippets. The web reader opened `ping` and `para`; after reader
errors, direct read-only HTTPS retrieval successfully obtained the actual
`gunggung`, `para-paran`, and `diping` pages. Nothing was downloaded to disk.
The two extra headword checks were limited to the current draft forms
`para-paran` and `diping`, whose exact mathematical forms were not found in the
initial headword entries. Do not interpret fuzzy query results as proof that a
word is impossible or absent from all Javanese usage.

Quotation totals below stay below 25 words from each cited page. These are
lexical attestations, not evidence of a standardized mathematics curriculum,
regional universality, or a tested pronunciation model.

## 1. ping: multiplication family is attested

Primary URL: [KBJI: ping](https://kbji.kemendikdasmen.go.id/kata/ping).

Short source quotations:

> ngepingaké mengalikan, melipatkan, menggandakan;
> ping-pingan perkalian, perbanyakan, pergandaan

The base entry also concerns ordinal occurrence and repetition; its meaning
must be selected from context. The derivative `ping-pingan` explicitly supports
the current multiplication-operation noun. It is no longer necessary to call
that noun an unattested invention. The exact formula-reading template
`a ping b`, however, is our context-bound pedagogical application, not an
example sentence quoted from this entry. No special academic register for that
template is established here.

Recommendation: retain `Ping-pingan` and the transparent `a ping b` reading,
with separate statuses for attested lexical family and provisional full
formula narration. Keep the distinction between a multiplication glyph and
the letter x. This is an inference for the current lesson, not a universal
parsing rule.

## 2. para: division, its calculation, and long division are attested

Primary URL: [KBJI: para](https://kbji.kemendikdasmen.go.id/kata/para).

Short source quotations from the second numbered sense and its subentries:

> para KN bagi, dibagi;
> para gapit, maragapit rumus pembagian bersusun;
> paran hitungan tentang membagi

The first numbered sense is a collective marker; several other homographs
are unrelated. The mathematical sense, not the first displayed homograph,
supports this lesson. The entry also supplies active/passive derivatives;
it does not provide the complete proposed sentence `a dipara b`.

Recommendation: replace the currently unattested noun `Para-paran` with
`Paran` in the operation-name column, subject to educator review of readability.
Use `para gapit` for the long-division method. A descriptive phrase such as
`kurung para gapit` still combines an attested method name with our diagram
wording; that whole phrase is not quoted attestation. Retain explicit
dividend/divisor descriptions. `a dipara b` remains a reasonable declared
morphological application, not a dictionary-certified formula template.
The proposed quotient label `asil paran` is likewise a compositional choice.

## 3. gunggung: the sum/total sense is directly attested

Primary URL: [KBJI: gunggung](https://kbji.kemendikdasmen.go.id/kata/gunggung).

Short source quotations:

> gunggung KN jumlah, total;
> nggunggung menjumlah;
> gunggungan jumlahan, totalan

The page separately lists a homographic persuasion/flattery sense. Use the
quantity sense in arithmetic and money subtotals, not the unrelated sense.
The page's actual HTML expands the label KN as “Kromo Ngoko”; this label does
not certify every drafted sentence's social appropriateness or academic style.

Recommendation: retain `Gunggung` for the table sum label and `gunggung` for
the banknote total; revise the evidence status from unverified lexical choice
to attested sum/total sense. A construction such as `gunggung a lan b` remains
our mathematical application. For the banknote instruction, explicitly total
all notes within each denomination before combining those subtotals; the
dictionary entry does not itself establish that instructional wording.

## Two bounded exact-form checks

- [KBJI: para-paran](https://kbji.kemendikdasmen.go.id/kata/para-paran)
  returned unrelated approximate matches, not an exact mathematical entry.
  This is limited negative lookup evidence. Prefer the attested division-family
  noun above, without claiming the original draft form is necessarily invalid.
- [KBJI: diping](https://kbji.kemendikdasmen.go.id/kata/diping)
  returned other words containing similar character sequences, not an exact
  multiplication entry. Do not describe `diping` as dictionary-confirmed.
  For consistency with A10, consider changing A00 money-label narration from
  `3 diping $100` to `3 ping $100`; this is a consistency recommendation,
  not proof that the inflected form is linguistically wrong.
