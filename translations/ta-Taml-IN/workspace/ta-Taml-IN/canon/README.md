# Readable Tamil canon - initial 12 examples

Reference: Government of Tamil Nadu / SCERT, Class 6, Term 1 Mathematics, first edition 2018. [School-hosted PDF mirror](https://www.kulikaraighss.in/books/6t1all/6t1maths.pdf). Publisher and edition were read on PDF pages 1-2, after OCR. This is a register/convention reference, not the translation source or a claim that the 2018 book is today's syllabus. The full PDF, page PNGs and OCR stay in ignored `downloads/tamil-canon/`.

Read OCR first, then check the page image whenever symbols, spelling or alignment matter. Tesseract Tamil OCR confuses mathematical operators and English; never adopt an equation from OCR without visual checking. The PNG inspection of PDF page 35 confirmed the number-set definitions. PDF page 175 separates whole numbers from integers in its glossary. Do not silently promote OCR noise into terminology.

2. During revision, compare actual target wording with the relevant example, not merely the ledger. Log decisions, disagreements and gaps in CONSULTATION_LOG.md.
3. Before build/QA, reread relevant examples and the source subsection; verify numbers against the image/source XML and update the stage log. Do not repeat the entire corpus ritualistically.
