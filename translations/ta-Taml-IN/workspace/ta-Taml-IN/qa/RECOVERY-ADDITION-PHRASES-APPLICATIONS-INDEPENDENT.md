## Source semantics and provenance

The source/domain boundary is honest and exact.

- U012/`fs-id2691382` is addition phrase → mathematical notation and result. The English and Indonesian witnesses both map “8 more than 7” / `8 lebih banyak dari 7` to `7+8`, and “6 added to 4” / `6 ditambahkan pada 4` to `4+6`. The Tamil source carries those mappings as `8 கூடுதலாக உள்ள எண், 7-உடன் ஒப்பிடும்போது` → `7+8` and `6 சேர்க்கப்பட்ட 4` → `4+6`. The companion quotes exactly these two mappings, labels them as the only source-derived phrase examples, and preserves semantic base/addend order while explaining that commutativity does not erase the wording relationship.
- U013/`fs-id2197427` defines the source plan: identify what is sought, write a phrase, translate it to notation, add/simplify, and answer in a sentence with the counted object or unit. It then applies that plan to totals and defines perimeter as the sum of side lengths. The companion's five checkpoints, final answer sentences, linear units, and perimeter/area contrast preserve that domain without copying or replacing a source exercise.
- The root marker `data-strand="original-companion"`, opening disclosure, and repeated route labels make all D/P/M/T questions and R1–R3 models newly authored. No source ID, source answer, figure, unit, or exercise is silently replaced.
- Required D/P/M/T work is addition. The only MathML subtraction is `82−47=35` inside the explicitly optional missing-addend/count-up check. That paragraph says it is not U012 source and not a required D/P/M/T step, limits the method to final amount ≥ starting amount, and restates addition phrase → notation/result as U012's primary route. The surrounding mention that subtraction is order-sensitive is explanatory contrast, not a routed subtraction lesson.

I found no meaning-changing or materially unidiomatic Tamil in the bounded text. `தொடக்க அளவு`, `சேரும் அளவு`, `மொத்தம்`, `எதைக் காண வேண்டும்`, `விடை வாக்கியம்`, `சுற்றளவு`, `எல்லையின் நீளம்`, and the linear-unit sentences remain clear and internally consistent. This is a model judgement, not a native-speaker sign-off.
