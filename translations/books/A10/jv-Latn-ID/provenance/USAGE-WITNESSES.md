## A real mathematical usage witness

Ririn Aprianita, *Sinau Matematika Kanthi Petungan Jawa = Belajar Matematika dengan Petungan Jawa* (Balai Bahasa Provinsi Daerah Istimewa Yogyakarta, 2023), ISBN 978-623-112-585-9. The official repository describes a bilingual Javanese/Indonesian children's book. [Official record](https://repositori.kemendikdasmen.go.id/32491/), [publisher PDF](https://repositori.kemendikdasmen.go.id/32491/1/86.%20Sinau%20Matematika%20Kanthi%20Petungan%20Jawa.pdf).

On printed page 12, the Javanese dialogue explicitly links “perkalian utawa ping-pingan” and “para-paranan utawa pembagian.” These short quotations total six words. The passage explains multiplication and division as related operations in a mathematical teaching context. This is evidence of actual mathematical usage, not just a dictionary definition. The parallel Indonesian column helps identify the intended mathematical sense, but is not counted as independent Javanese evidence.

## Canon distinction

The pinned shared canon remains in `shared-canon-README.md` and `shared-canon-sources.lock.json`, with the unchanged inherited `shared-terminology.csv`. The recovery consulted the canon's explicit scope and decisions for C01 wilangan, C14 owah, C15 tetep, C21 ping, C22 para, C29 kurung, C30 tutup, C34 rambang, C43 ganti, C46 suku, and C47 jinis.

The canon itself says that C46 does not give an algebraic definition of `suku`, and C47's ordinary category sense does not attest the compound `suku sajinis`. This edition therefore keeps `suku`, `koefisien`, `variabel`, and `konstanta` as declared source-defined school loans, and `suku sajinis` as a provisional mathematical compound. It does not claim that the cited dictionary or the new children's reader attests these algebraic compounds.

The source defines a term, a coefficient, equal variable/exponent structure, and the rule for combining like terms. That source-bound mathematical definition is the reason for the term choices. The separate Indonesian bridge is a meaning comparison, not proof of native Javanese usage.
