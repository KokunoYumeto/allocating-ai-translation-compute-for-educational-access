## What the counts mean

A full source draft covers a canonical module; it is not automatically a fully reviewed learner product. A short chapter opener is not equivalent in volume to an instructional module. An excerpt reader, translation track, generated page, checksum or test does not count as another complete module. Independently authored answers and learning companions are distinct from supplied source solutions.

Readiness is per artifact and per revision. Existing mathematical/structural or bounded visual receipts do not imply native educator approval, classroom validation, assistive-technology certification or exhaustive device testing. Pending human review does not stop continued drafting. A PDF check does not clear changed HTML, and SSML does not establish produced or listened-to audio.
