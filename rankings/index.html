What remains before a defensible allocation recommendation

The study still has to compare exact educational packages at the relevant stage and subject; measure independently usable existing supply and additional learning effect; account for offline, phone, signed-language and other accessibility delivery; avoid double-counting multilingual and displaced beneficiaries; count shared source preparation and interlanguage reuse once; and convert workload into standardized physical compute. Until then, these are transparent reach and common-package workload comparisons—not a final instruction to spend money in rank order.
