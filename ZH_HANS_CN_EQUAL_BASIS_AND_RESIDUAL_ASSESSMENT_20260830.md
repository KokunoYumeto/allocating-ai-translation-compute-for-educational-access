# Mainland Simplified Chinese: equal-basis opportunity and forward residual

Date: 2026-08-30  
Target profile: `zh-Hans-CN`  
Territory: Chinese mainland, excluding Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan in the population denominator

## Corrected conclusion

The previous Top-100 omission of `zh-Hans-CN` was methodologically inconsistent. A
completed or partial local edition can reduce forward deficit and cost; it cannot erase
the language population from the ex-ante question, "Where would this language have
ranked if every candidate had been considered on the same basis?"

This assessment therefore creates two rows that must never be conflated:

1. **Equal-basis opportunity row:** `D = 1` for the fixed FR-2 comparator, exactly as
   for every other natural-language candidate. This is the ex-ante language-selection
   counterfactual.
2. **Forward residual row:** `D = 0` for exact completed Open Logic and *Algebra and
   Trigonometry 2e* content; Calculus Volume 1 has 29 of 55 modules translated and 26
   pending at the pinned state; the six other books in the fixed eight-book foundational
   STEM collection remain pending. Actual forward compute must use measured residual
   source burden rather than a gross population multiplier.

## Population basis and limits

The National Bureau of Statistics reports 1,411,778,724 persons in the 31 mainland
provincial-level jurisdictions plus servicemen at the 1 November 2020 census. This is a
gross territory ceiling, not an exact count of people who read standardized Simplified
Chinese comfortably.

The Ministry of Education reports that, by 2020, Putonghua prevalence was 80.72% and
more than 95% of the literate population used standardized Chinese characters. It also
reports large geographic inequality: nearly one fifth of the population could not
communicate in Putonghua, nearly half of provincial-level areas were below 85%, and the
lowest and highest areas differed by almost 50 percentage points. These observations
support `zh-Hans-CN` as a very large written-standard target while forbidding the paper
from relabeling the full territory population as an exact comfortable-reader count.

## Existing supply

China has abundant Chinese-language education resources. The national higher-education
platform describes 31,000 high-quality online courses and more than 65,000 other higher-
education resources across 14 discipline categories and 92 professional categories,
free to university users and social learners. A 2025 Ministry of Education account
reported 110,000 K-12 resources, more than 11,000 vocational courses, and 31,000 higher-
education online courses on the national public service platform. Abundance means that
a generic "Chinese has no textbooks" premise is false.

The marginal intervention is instead a permanent, openly redistributable,
teacher-independent, offline, accessible, and internally sequenced corpus, plus exact
advanced or research-frontier gaps. Platform availability, free access, open licensing,
downloadability, exercise/answer completeness, and semantic accessibility are different
properties and must not be treated as interchangeable.

## Recommended commissioning sequence

1. Do not retranslate completed Open Logic or *Algebra and Trigonometry 2e*.
2. Complete Calculus Volume 1 from its exact 26-module residual, then continue the
   fixed STEM collection only where the edition remains absent.
3. Produce semantic HTML/MathML, navigable EPUB, and tagged PDF derivatives for the
   complete Open Logic corpus; verify mathematical navigation and Unicode extraction.
4. Audit the gap between the completed foundation and postgraduate/research-frontier
   needs. Commission exact missing corpora, terminology crosswalks, exercises, and
   prerequisite bridges rather than assuming that national platform abundance closes
   every advanced gap.
5. Keep minority-language, regional, and non-Putonghua access separate. `zh-Hans-CN`
   does not automatically cover every Sinitic language or every language community in
   China.
