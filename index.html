This edition has been withdrawn

Do not use the 4 September 2026 edition to allocate funding or translation work.

Its ordinal table admitted a Russian-language cohort using a raw language-ability count and generic assumptions for educational mismatch, resource scarcity, and academic-language access. Standard Ukrainian was left outside that table because the evidence pipeline had not bound a resident-language denominator. The result therefore reflected unequal evidence availability, not a defensible comparison of educational need.

The underlying files remain available in repository history for transparent audit. A replacement analysis must not award high ranks from generic assumptions, must not make major populations disappear when evidence is incomplete, and must keep unsupported comparisons out of funding recommendations.

Withdrawal recorded 4 September 2026.
