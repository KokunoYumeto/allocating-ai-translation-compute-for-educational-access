# Bahasa Indonesia equal-basis correction

The earlier headline ranking excluded Bahasa Indonesia because the candidate register
classified it only as an existing partial-edition completion. That was a category
error: it made prior production a reason to omit the language from the ex-ante
comparison applied to missing-language candidates. A second, now superseded task-local
claim described the Open Logic program as 321 of 722 modules and modeled a remaining
completion workload. Current program authority states that 722 of 722 Open Logic
modules exist and that the Indonesian mathematics program comprises approximately
25,000 pages. The exact broader-program page total and public catalog remain subject to
a bounded inventory audit; this correction does not claim every broader-program volume
is complete.

The corrected analysis separates two estimands:

1. **Ex-ante language opportunity.** Use the same complete FR-2/D3 comparator and
   `D=1` used for every other natural-language candidate. The official BPS 2022 table
   reports 248,501,794 people aged 5+ able to speak Indonesian. With the already
   registered neutral sensitivities `P=0.96` and `U=0.5`, the base marginal-access
   sensitivity is 119,280,861 and base efficiency is 29,285,398 people per million
   gross tokens. This row remains portfolio position 1. It answers whether Bahasa
   Indonesia was a strong choice before candidate-specific production was credited.
2. **Ex-post Open Logic allocation.** The current Open Logic completion deficit is
   `D=0`. Consequently, the remaining Open Logic translation workload, modeled gross
   tokens, marginal-access increment from additional Open Logic translation, and
   translation-completion efficiency are all zero. This does not mean the existing
   program has zero educational value; it means that duplicating already completed
   Open Logic translation creates no further translation-completion benefit.

No specific next content package follows from the obsolete partial-program model. A
bounded catalog-and-gap audit must first establish what the current Indonesian program
does not contain. Accessibility, offline delivery, diagnostics, QA, maintenance, or a
specifically uncovered advanced topic may still be valuable, but must be evaluated as
separate interventions rather than mislabeled as Open Logic completion.

Neither the ex-ante sensitivity nor the completed-baseline row is an observed number of
people newly educated. BPS measures oral functional Indonesian ability, not written
literacy or academic comfort. The zero lower bound, 96% territory literacy proxy, and
50% neutral academic-language non-overlap sensitivity remain explicit.

Primary population source: BPS-Statistics Indonesia, *Jumlah Penduduk Berumur 5 Tahun
ke Atas Menurut Wilayah, Jenis Kelamin, dan Kemampuan Berbahasa Indonesia*, 2022,
<https://sensus.bps.go.id/topik/tabular/sp2022/196> (`PM-S021`). Current program-status
authority and its evidence boundary are preserved in
`CURRENT_INDONESIAN_PROGRAM_STATUS_20260830.md`.
