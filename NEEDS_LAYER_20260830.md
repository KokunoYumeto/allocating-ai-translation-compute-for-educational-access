# Population-specific mathematics needs layer

## Decision rule

Need is estimated for a language x territory x learner stage x delivery context, not once per language. Several tiers can coexist. The first commission addresses the lowest demonstrated binding prerequisite for a measured cohort, while a separate advanced allocation remains permissible where a specialist cohort and a source gap are documented. Missing evidence is unknown, never zero.

The evidence supports four materially different first-product strategies:

- foundational recovery: Bangladesh Bangla, Indian Bengali, Telugu, Indian Tamil, Gujarati, and the broad-access Bahasa Indonesia lane;
- bilingual transfer scaffolding: Javanese into Bahasa Indonesia and Telugu into later English-medium STEM terminology;
- secondary-to-undergraduate bridging: Marathi and Western Punjabi/Shahmukhi;
- coherent undergraduate spine: Vietnamese;
- advanced prerequisite/reference ladder: Japanese negative-control case.

These are marginal allocations. They do not claim that advanced work in a foundational-recovery language is useless, or that a population with strong school outcomes has no learners below the baseline.

## Product ladder

| Tier | Activating evidence | First open product |
|---|---|---|
| Foundational recovery | Large measured arithmetic/numeracy deficit; absence of independently usable feedback; language or register barrier | Diagnostic modules from number sense through fractions and prealgebra, complete worked solutions, cumulative review, and printable/offline derivatives |
| Secondary bridge | Foundational readiness is plausible or embedded remediation can address it; functions, algebra, trigonometry, data or academic-language transition is the binding gap | Elementary Algebra/Algebra 1 through Algebra and Trigonometry/Precalculus, with bilingual terminology where later STEM uses another language |
| Undergraduate core | Named tertiary demand and prerequisite readiness; fragmented rather than absent local content | Coherent precalculus-calculus-linear-algebra-discrete/proof spine with course graph, diagnostics, hints and full solutions |
| Advanced/reference | Named graduate/research cohort or an established specialist corpus gap | Dependency path first: proof, abstract algebra, commutative algebra, then algebraic geometry or another specialist reference |

OpenStax and Open Logic are source baselines, not a rule that each population receives the same book. The common FR-2 and Algebra-and-Trigonometry scenarios remain useful compute comparators because their source sizes are measured. They are not population-specific content recommendations.

## Teacher-independent delivery baseline

Each selected package should be usable by a motivated learner who can obtain it once and may have no teacher or continuous connection. The minimum bundle is editable open source, semantic HTML/MathML or EPUB, screen and grayscale-print PDFs, a prerequisite map, placement tests, exercises, hints, public final answers, worked solutions/error explanations, attribution, version and hashes. Video and live links are optional derivatives. Shared-device use, low storage, and a single downloadable archive are first-order design requirements rather than afterthoughts.

Open licensing is a necessary program property here: permission to translate, adapt, repair, redistribute, print and preserve is what makes a global many-language workflow possible. A free-to-view portal is evidence of supply but not automatically an open adaptation source.

## Evidence limits

- ASER India results are rural state proxies, not direct samples of language speakers or urban populations. The derived `at least subtraction` rate adds mutually exclusive subtraction and division categories.
- Pakistan Punjab is a Shahmukhi/Urdu/English education context; Indian Gurmukhi Punjabi is not an interchangeable output.
- Indonesia's PISA result is not a Javanese-specific mathematics measure.
- PISA covers enrolled 15-year-old samples and reports explicit cohort coverage; it is not a whole-population learning rate.
- Portal inventories establish known supply, not exhaustive absence. A missing or ambiguous license remains unknown.
- The detailed machine-readable authority, scope, recommendation, and caveat for every audited row is in `population_mathematics_needs_register.csv`.
