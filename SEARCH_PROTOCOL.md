# Evidence and search protocol

## Question

Which bounded language, interlanguage, or accessibility intervention applied to
which OpenStax or Open Logic curriculum unit creates the largest additional
usable educational access per unit of AI compute, without double-counting people
already comfortably served?

## Unit of analysis

Every population observation is keyed as:

`language variety × territory × script/orthography × learner stratum × date`.

Every intervention is keyed as:

`target register/format × curriculum work/unit × adaptation depth × release format`.

Macro-labels (for example, Quechua, Maya, Arabic, Chinese, Cree, Ojibwe,
Inuktut, or sign language) are discovery labels only. They are never population
or coverage units unless a cited source itself measures that aggregate and the
paper preserves its components and limitations.

## Source hierarchy

1. Current official censuses, education ministries, statistical agencies,
   standards bodies, and curriculum/OER publishers.
2. Peer-reviewed language-demography, language-of-instruction,
   intelligibility, literacy, OER, and translation-economics research.
3. Reputable institutional datasets with documented provenance (World Bank,
   UNESCO, UNICEF, UIS, OECD, national academies, CLARIN and comparable bodies).
4. Linguistic catalogues and community/standardization institutions for
   identifiers, varieties, scripts, orthographies, and vitality; catalogue
   speaker figures remain dated estimates rather than timeless facts.
5. Secondary sources only as locators or context where a primary source is
   obtainable.

## Mandatory fields for a population claim

- Exact variety name and stable identifier(s), where available.
- Territory and whether the figure is national, regional, or diasporic.
- L1, L2, home-language, usual-language, comprehension, or ethnic-identity
  status; these categories are never silently combined.
- Numerator, denominator, reference year, source date, and source table/page.
- Script and educational orthography actually used in the territory.
- Low/base/high range and a confidence grade when sources disagree.
- Whether the same people are plausibly counted in another candidate cell.

## Educational-access evidence

Language-of-instruction mismatch, advanced-resource scarcity, connectivity,
disability/accessibility, and academic-lingua-franca comfort are separate
variables. A national or official language does not establish comfortable
access. Conversely, an L1 population does not establish that every speaker is
educationally underserved.

Evidence of scarcity is recorded by curriculum level and domain:

- foundational literacy/numeracy;
- secondary mathematics/science;
- postsecondary gateway courses represented by OpenStax;
- formal logic and mathematical logic represented by Open Logic;
- accessible/offline forms of already translated material.

## Interlanguage evidence rule

An interlanguage receives coverage credit only for population cells supported
by direct or experimentally grounded comprehension evidence, or by a narrowly
bounded inference from measured adjacent varieties with an explicit penalty.
The record must state script, prior-study requirement, receptive versus
productive comprehension, education level, tested task, confidence interval,
and exclusions. A project name, genealogical relation, or vocabulary overlap
never establishes usable comprehension.

## Inclusion and negative controls

Candidate generation is global and includes both large underserved languages
and small/endangered prestige-domain interventions. Negative controls include
languages with strong open advanced-material provision or high overlap with an
already available edition. They remain in the table so the model can show why
their incremental value is lower.

## Reproducibility and uncertainty

- Preserve raw reported quantities before harmonization.
- Never back-solve unnamed languages from an aggregate table.
- Report intervals and sensitivity, not false point precision.
- Calculate both unweighted newly comfortable readers and separately labeled
  scarcity-, equity-, vitality/prestige-, and feasibility-weighted outcomes.
- Publish ranking ties and unstable positions as bands.
- Retain citations at the row and edge level so every score can be recomputed.
