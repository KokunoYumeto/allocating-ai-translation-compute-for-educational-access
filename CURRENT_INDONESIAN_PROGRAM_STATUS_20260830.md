> “722 out of 722 OpenLogic modules exist, like 25,000 pages of a full mathematics curriculum exist.”

> “There is no Indonesian pilot. There is an Indonesian program.”

These statements supersede the earlier task-local descriptions of the Indonesian work as a “pilot,” as “partial,” or as 321 of 722 Open Logic modules.

## Status used in this research model

- **Program status:** established Indonesian mathematics program, not a pilot.
- **Open Logic status:** 722 of 722 modules exist. The forward Open Logic translation-completion deficit is therefore `D=0`.
- **Broader scale:** the bounded public audit at program commit `a6ae2e3c9a1abb77132a0028414780c9976e48e4` verifies 27 of 40 roles published and 13 in production, with no unresolved role. A pinned public-artifact reconstruction totals 26,031 de-duplicated rendered pages. The central page ledger separately records 19,745 measured teaching-package pages, 20,763 selected-corpus working pages, and a 27,705-page documented rendered universe (including donor and Stacks reference pages).
- **Open Logic build boundary:** commit `07b25e1329a95a0ace266533f32f3671c2cef95e` contains exactly 722 manifest rows. The 1,116-page canonical reader reaches 642 modules; 80 additional translated targets are non-reader source modules retained in the editable package.
- **Evidence boundary:** these are public repository and exact-ledger facts reproduced in `INDONESIAN_PUBLIC_PROGRAM_AUDIT_20260830.md` and `INDONESIAN_PROGRAM_COMPUTE_AND_PAGE_RECONCILIATION_20260830.md`. They establish an approximately 25,000-page rendered/corpus scale, while also showing that 13 course roles remain in production. None of the page universes is a final translated-page total.
- **Ex-ante result:** the equal-basis counterfactual is unchanged. Bahasa Indonesia ranks first under the paper’s equal-treatment opportunity calculation.
- **Ex-post allocation result:** already delivered Open Logic material creates no remaining Open Logic translation-completion workload. It is a completed baseline, not a forward translation candidate.
- **Next-content rule:** no specific next textbook, course, or bridge may be named from the obsolete partial-program assumption. A bounded catalog-and-gap audit must first identify content genuinely absent from the current program. Accessibility, offline delivery, diagnostics, QA, maintenance, or an uncovered advanced topic may still create marginal value, but each requires its own demonstrated gap.
