## Research and production chain

The model performed source discovery, evidence reconciliation, exact-profile
definition, literature synthesis, population and curriculum crosswalk construction,
model specification, scenario analysis, drafting, mathematical and terminological
sense-checking, consistency review, code generation, deterministic QA, and document
production. It did not treat generic machine translation as a substitute for
mathematical interpretation. Formula/structure preservation, terminology evidence,
source identity, cross-table consistency, and independent model critique were used
as separable checks.

The paper's conclusions are generated from registered public evidence and frozen
model inputs. Missing human evidence never becomes a hidden zero or a publication
hold: uncertainty is reported as an interval, evidence label, noncardinal status, or
explicitly unresolved field. This does not convert model output into observed reader
comprehension.

## Deterministic validation

The public-content validator checks row counts, unique portfolio positions, Top 10
order, 100/100 needs assignments, the Top-100 interlanguage crosswalk, compute
accounting identities, required manuscript claims, forbidden private paths and
placeholders, and SHA-256 identities for key public files. The document builder uses
`PAPER.md` as its source of truth and records a build report. The DOCX audit checks
semantic text round-trip, section and table structure, styles, accessibility
properties, controlling-model identity, and rendered-page anomalies. PDF validation
checks page count, extraction, layout, and final-file identity.

Any content or data change invalidates downstream hashes and requires regeneration of
the affected tables, manuscript formats, figure, manifest, and QA receipts. Public
publication is complete only after anonymous readback proves filenames, byte counts,
and SHA-256 identity from both standalone destinations.

## Interpretive boundary

The model estimates marginal educational-access opportunities and production
workloads under explicit assumptions. It does not measure realized enrollment,
learning gains, comprehension, teacher availability, device ownership, or adoption.
Its population ceilings, sensitivity values, and scarcity proxies must not be
presented as observed harmed-person counts.

Constructed interlanguages and shared-core architectures receive no blanket regional
reach. Advanced mathematical notation and reader expertise may make receptive bridge
use more plausible than in novice prose, but cross-language demographic credit remains
zero until exact-profile evidence supports it. This preserves an empirical research
question without declaring the architecture useless.
