# Japanese educational-access assessment: stage- and content-specific correction

Date: 2026-08-30  
Target profile: `ja-Jpan-JP`  
Status: evidence-backed calibration assessment; not a cardinal population estimate

## Corrected conclusion

Japanese is not a language-wide negative control. The available evidence supports a
much narrower conclusion: another generic compulsory-school algebra edition is likely
to have low marginal value relative to under-supplied populations, because represented
Japanese 15-year-olds perform strongly in PISA mathematics and compulsory-school
textbooks are supplied to enrolled pupils at national expense. Those facts do not prove
that Japanese readers have complete, open-licensed, teacher-independent, accessible, or
research-frontier mathematical coverage.

The defensible allocation treatment is therefore stage-specific:

1. **Compulsory-school duplicate:** deprioritize unless an exact accessibility,
   self-study, or open-license deficit is demonstrated.
2. **Undergraduate transition:** retain a targeted proof-writing -> abstract algebra ->
   commutative algebra -> algebraic geometry sequence, with terminology crosswalks,
   exercises, and full solutions.
3. **Postgraduate and research-frontier:** retain translations and critical editions of
   major source corpora where the exact Japanese gap is demonstrated. Specialist reach
   and research-capacity value must be reported separately from mass foundational reach.
4. **Accessibility conversion:** treat semantic HTML/MathML, navigable EPUB, tagged PDF,
   structured audio, and low-bandwidth/offline derivatives as a separate intervention.

## Evidence

- OECD PISA 2022 reports that 88% of represented Japanese 15-year-olds reached at
  least Level 2 in mathematics and 23% reached Levels 5-6. This is evidence about the
  represented school-age cohort, not adult, independent-study, disability, open-license,
  postgraduate, or research-frontier access.
- MEXT states that textbooks for compulsory education are supplied to all pupils free
  of charge at national expense. The same page states that textbooks are basically
  authored and edited by private publishers and authorized for school use. Free supply
  to enrolled pupils is not evidence that source files are openly licensed for global
  reuse, adaptation, or indefinite offline redistribution.
- JMOOC reports 779 certified courses, about 1.65 million unique registered learners,
  and 1.87 million course enrollments as of March 2025. This demonstrates meaningful
  Japanese online higher/adult provision, while registration and platform delivery do
  not establish a complete reusable mathematics curriculum.
- *SUGAKU*, published by the Mathematical Society of Japan, reported 3,058 registered
  articles on 27 January 2026 and describes itself as peer-reviewed and free access.
  This is strong evidence of a live Japanese advanced-mathematics ecosystem; it is not
  an inventory of textbooks, licenses, exercises, or accessible formats.
- Kyoto University OCW provides Japanese undergraduate lectures on representation
  theory, formal power series, Artin approximation, and algebraic-geometric aspects of
  power series. Nagoya University OCW provides Japanese notes and practice problems on
  sets, maps, vector spaces, and homomorphisms designed for self-study. The Mathematical
  Society of Japan's 2026 algebra symposium explicitly includes algebraic geometry,
  ring theory, number theory, group theory, and representation theory. These examples
  disprove any claim that advanced Japanese mathematics is absent, but they do not prove
  sequence completeness or coverage of major translated research corpora.
- The National Diet Library's Mina Search indexes braille, DAISY, EPUB, text, large
  print, and other accessible formats. Download access to some disability-service data
  requires registration. No source audited here establishes complete semantic-math or
  navigational coverage for advanced Japanese mathematics.

## Population and ranking treatment

No exact national Japanese-language comfort count was established in this bounded pass.
Japan's total population would be a territory ceiling, not an exact language count.
Accordingly, this correction removes the unsupported language-wide negative-control
label but does not manufacture a cardinal reach value. Japanese remains eligible for
stage-specific, non-cardinal commissioning until a suitable exact population/profile
observation and content inventory are joined.

## First nonduplicative package

The first package is not another school algebra text. It is a bilingual-terminology,
teacher-independent proof-to-algebra bridge with complete worked exercises and offline
semantic formats, followed by commutative algebra and algebraic geometry. A separate
audit should then identify exact Japanese-language absences in research-frontier corpora
such as EGA, SGA, FGA, seminar literature, and modern prerequisite monographs.
